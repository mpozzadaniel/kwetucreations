Need to Know?

Frequently Asked Questions

Have questions about our services or starting a project? Find answers to some common questions below.

Discuss Your Project
What services does Kwetu Creations offer?
We offer branding, signage, laser services, fabrication and interior solutions. Contact us to discuss your specific requirements.
How can I request a quote?
Visit our Request a Quote page and provide your project details and contact information so our team can review your enquiry.
Can I request a custom design?
Yes. Share your ideas, requirements, measurements and any reference images or design files when submitting your enquiry.
Can I provide my own design or artwork?
Yes. You can tell us about your existing artwork or upload relevant files through the quote request form, where supported.
How much do your services cost?
Pricing depends on the type of service, materials, dimensions, design requirements and project scope. Send us your details for a project-specific quote.
How long will my project take?
Completion time depends on the complexity and size of the project, materials required and production schedule. We can discuss the expected timeline when reviewing your enquiry.
How do I get started?
Start by submitting a quote request with a description of what you need. Include any useful reference images, measurements or files to help us understand your project.
