Please read these terms carefully

Please read these terms before sending an enquiry, accepting a quotation or starting a project. They set out how Kwetu Creations works with the businesses it serves. Specific prices, payment schedules, deadlines, warranties and project conditions are confirmed in the written quotation or agreement for your project, and that document governs where it says something different.

01 Introduction

These Terms & Conditions apply to enquiries, quotations and projects undertaken by Kwetu Creations, subject to any separate written agreement accepted by the parties.

A quotation or proposal may contain additional project-specific terms. If a separate written agreement conflicts with these general terms, the parties should clarify which terms apply before work begins.

No provision in these terms is intended to remove any right or protection that cannot lawfully be excluded under applicable law.

02 Our Services

Kwetu Creations provides creative and production services that may include:

  • Signage and business displays.
  • Branding and visual identity applications.
  • Interior branding and interior solutions.
  • Illuminated signs and related applications.
  • Custom fabrication and related project work.

The exact scope of each project will be set out in the relevant quotation, proposal or written agreement. Any service not expressly included should be confirmed separately.

03 Quotations & Pricing

A quotation will be based on the project information available at the time it is prepared. Accurate measurements, specifications, quantities, materials and installation requirements help us provide an appropriate estimate.

Unless a quotation states otherwise, it should be treated as a proposal rather than a guarantee that work has been booked or production has begun. A project is scheduled after the scope, price, relevant terms and any required initial payment have been confirmed.

Each quotation should state its own validity period. If no period is stated, the validity should be confirmed with Kwetu Creations before the client accepts it.

If the project scope, specifications, quantities, materials, site conditions or other requirements change, the price and timeline may need to be reviewed. Any agreed adjustment should be documented before the additional work proceeds.

04 Deposits & Payments

The required deposit, payment milestones, balance and payment methods should be stated in the accepted quotation or written agreement.

Work may be scheduled to begin once the agreed acceptance requirements and initial payment have been satisfied.

Where a payment becomes overdue, the parties should communicate promptly to resolve the situation. Any lawful pause in work, revised delivery date, additional cost or other consequence should follow the agreed terms and applicable law.

Clients should retain payment confirmations and request a receipt or other appropriate acknowledgement for payments made.

We do not hold a single standard deposit or payment schedule across the studio, because a roll-up banner and a fitted interior are not the same commitment. Whatever applies to your project is written into the quotation you accept, before work is scheduled, and the written quotation is what the team works to.

05 Design & Approval

Where a project includes design work, the client should review the proposed artwork, layout, dimensions, wording, colours, materials and other relevant details before approving production.

The client is responsible for carefully checking supplied names, telephone numbers, addresses, spelling, measurements and other information before approval.

Production or fabrication should not proceed until the required approval has been received and any agreed pre-production requirements have been met.

Changes requested after approval may affect the cost and schedule. These changes should be discussed and approved before implementation.

06 Changes & Revisions

The quotation should state how many design concepts or revision rounds are included, where relevant.

A revision is a change to an existing design within the agreed project scope. A substantially different concept, additional deliverable, new specification or change in project scope may require a revised quotation.

Before carrying out chargeable additional work, Kwetu Creations should explain the proposed cost and any effect on the delivery date and obtain the client's agreement.

07 Cancellations & Refunds

If a client wishes to cancel or suspend a project, the request should be communicated in writing as soon as possible.

The parties should review work already completed, materials ordered, fabrication undertaken, third-party commitments and other reasonable project costs incurred up to the cancellation date.

Any amount payable, refund or other adjustment should be assessed under the agreed project terms and applicable law. No deposit should automatically be treated as non-refundable unless the relevant condition has been properly agreed and is lawful.

Kwetu Creations should communicate the proposed settlement clearly and provide an appropriate breakdown where necessary.

08 Production, Installation & Delivery

Production and delivery estimates depend on factors such as design approval, material availability, project complexity, site access and installation requirements.

Any agreed target date should be confirmed in the quotation or project communication. If a significant delay becomes apparent, the parties should communicate and agree on the next steps.

Where installation is included, the quotation should identify the relevant location, scope of work, access requirements and any responsibilities assigned to the client.

Permits, site preparation, electrical work, structural work, transport or other additional services should be clearly allocated in the project agreement rather than assumed to be included.

The client should ensure that the agreed site is accessible and that required permissions are obtained by the party responsible for them.

09 Client Responsibilities

To help a project proceed smoothly, the client should, where applicable:

  • Provide accurate project information, measurements and specifications.
  • Supply artwork and materials they are authorised to use.
  • Review and approve designs within agreed timelines.
  • Make payments in accordance with the accepted agreement.
  • Arrange access to the project site where required.
  • Obtain permissions or approvals assigned to the client.
  • Communicate changes or concerns promptly.

If missing information, delayed approvals, restricted site access or other client-related issues affect the work, the parties should discuss any reasonable adjustment to the schedule or scope.

10 Intellectual Property & Project Materials

Clients retain whatever rights they hold in their original logos, artwork, photographs and other materials supplied to Kwetu Creations. By supplying those materials, clients confirm that they are authorised to provide them for the agreed project.

Ownership and permitted use of new designs, working files, source files, production files and other deliverables should be specified in the accepted quotation or written agreement.

Unless expressly agreed otherwise, clients should not assume that payment for a finished product automatically includes every editable source file or the transfer of all intellectual property rights.

Kwetu Creations should obtain appropriate permission before using identifiable client work, confidential designs or project photographs in marketing or portfolio materials where required.

11 Quality, Defects & After-Sales Support

The agreed specifications and any applicable legal standards will guide the expected quality of the work.

If a client believes that delivered work does not match the agreed specifications, they should notify Kwetu Creations promptly and provide relevant information or photographs where appropriate.

We will review the concern and discuss reasonable next steps in accordance with the agreement and applicable law.

Any product warranty, workmanship warranty, maintenance arrangement or exclusions should be expressly stated in the relevant quotation or written agreement. This page does not establish a specific warranty period.

12 Liability & Unforeseen Events

Each party should take reasonable steps to meet its agreed responsibilities and minimise avoidable losses.

If events outside a party's reasonable control materially affect a project, the affected party should notify the other party as soon as reasonably possible and discuss practical arrangements.

Nothing in these terms excludes or limits liability where such exclusion or limitation is prohibited by applicable law. Any agreed allocation or limitation of responsibility should be reviewed for legal enforceability.

13 Privacy & Personal Information

Information provided through enquiries, quotations and project communications will be handled in accordance with our Privacy Policy and applicable data-protection law.

For details about the intended collection, use, retention and protection of personal information, please read our Privacy Policy .

14 Complaints & Dispute Resolution

If a concern arises, the parties should first try to resolve it through clear and timely communication.

Where a concern cannot be resolved informally, the parties may consider an appropriate mediation or other lawful dispute-resolution process, if agreed or required.

These terms and any project agreement are subject to applicable law. Where a quotation names the law or court that governs it, that statement applies to that project.

15 Changes to These Terms

Kwetu Creations may update these general terms as its services, processes or legal obligations change.

The current version will be published on this page. Any changes affecting an existing project should be handled in accordance with the accepted agreement and applicable law.

Project-specific written terms should be reviewed carefully before a quotation or agreement is accepted.
