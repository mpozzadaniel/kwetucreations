The short version

This website does not plant advertising or tracking cookies on you. The storage it writes itself is two items: the small record of the choice you make in the consent bar, so that the bar does not follow you from page to page, and — only if you press submit on one of the forms — one technical cookie that lets the server recognise that submission. Neither is used for advertising, and neither is read back as a history of what you have looked at.

01 The Plain Answer

Cookies are small text files a website asks your browser to keep. Modern sites also use localStorage, which does the same job from inside the browser without being sent to the server on every request. Under cookie and e-privacy rules, both are treated the same way, so this page covers both.

Kwetu Creations writes two items of storage, both listed in full in the table below. Your consent record lives on your device and is never sent to us. The form cookie is issued by our own server, cannot be read by other scripts on the page, and is gone when you close the browser.

03 The Three Choices You Are Offered

Strictly necessary — always on

Your consent decision itself has to be remembered, otherwise the banner would reopen on every page you visit. A submitted form has to be recognised by the server, otherwise anyone's script could post enquiries through it. This is the only category that works without a choice from you, and it carries no personal profile: the consent record stores three yes or no values and a date, and the form cookie stores one random number.

Analytics — optional

Analytics would tell the studio which pages people read and which projects attract interest. This website runs none. The switch is here so that your decision is recorded first and asked for second: no measurement service can load for you unless you have said yes.

Marketing — optional

Marketing covers advertising cookies and social-media pixels used to follow visitors between websites or show them ads. This website uses neither, and the same rule applies: nothing in this category loads unless you have accepted it.

04 How The Consent Bar Works

  • It appears at the bottom of the first page you open.
  • Accept All records analytics and marketing as accepted.
  • Reject Non-Essential records both as refused and keeps only the necessary choice.
  • Cookie Settings opens the panel so you can decide category by category, then Save Preferences.
  • Whichever button you press, the bar closes and does not return until you reopen Cookie Settings.

No tracking script is loaded before you answer. This website carries no analytics or advertising code at all, so nothing in those categories can load after you answer either. Your recorded choice is what any future service would have to respect first, which is a safer arrangement than asking permission afterwards.

05 What This Website Does Not Do

  • No advertising networks, remarketing lists or social-media pixels.
  • No cross-website profiles and no sale of browsing behaviour.
  • No hidden tracking triggered by scrolling, clicking or hovering.
  • No analytics or advertising cookie attached to the enquiry and quote forms — only the one technical cookie listed in the inventory.
  • No analytics or advertising script of any kind, whether a category was accepted or refused.

The contact, quote request and testimonial forms now send their answers to this website's own intake system, which is described in the Privacy Policy. The request carries the cookie above so the server can tell it started on this site. That is the whole extent of what the forms do; nothing about you is watched before, during or after the submission, and no advertiser is told that you filled anything in.

06 Third-Party Requests That Are Not Cookies

Honest disclosure needs the whole picture, so these requests are named. They are not tracking cookies and they are not used to build a profile of you, but they do mean your browser contacts another company:

  • Google Fonts (fonts.googleapis.com and fonts.gstatic.com) supplies the Poppins typeface used on every page.
  • cdnjs (cdnjs.cloudflare.com) supplies the Font Awesome icon stylesheet.
  • Your email application opens when you use a mailto: link, and the footer designer credit can open a Gmail compose window if you click it.

Each of those requests carries your IP address to the provider, as any web request does, and each provider handles that request under its own privacy policy. They load because the design and the icons depend on them, not because of advertising.

Nothing is set by them for advertising. A font file and an icon sheet are static assets: the providers named above do not place a cookie on your device to follow you between websites.

07 Analytics and Advertising

This website runs no analytics service and no advertising or social-platform tracker. No page on it reports your visit to Google, Meta or any other measurement provider, and the only third-party requests an ordinary page view makes are the font and icon files listed above.

The consent panel still asks about Analytics and Marketing, and the answer you give is stored on your own device. Choosing first and being asked later is the safer arrangement: nothing in either category can load for you unless your record says yes, and a visitor who rejected it is never re-asked by a silent update.

08 Changing Your Choice

Your decision is never final. Cookie Settings sits in the footer of every page and reopens the panel, so you can turn analytics or marketing on or off, or withdraw it entirely, as often as you like. Saving replaces the previous record on your device immediately.

Clearing the site's stored data in your browser removes the record and brings the consent bar back on your next visit. That is a quick way to withdraw consent if you share a device.

09 Your Browser Controls

Every browser can block or delete cookies and site data, usually under a privacy or security menu. Blocking all storage is allowed and respected here: the website stays readable, and the consent bar may return on each visit because the browser refuses to remember the answer.

Browser extensions that block trackers also work against this site, because there is nothing of theirs to find.

10 Changes To This Cookie Policy

This page is written against the code that is actually in the website folder. If a tracker, a service or a storage item is added, the inventory in section 02 is updated first, the consent categories are reviewed, and the version inside the consent record is increased so that returning visitors are asked again.

Review this page when you next visit; it carries no promise of advance notice.
