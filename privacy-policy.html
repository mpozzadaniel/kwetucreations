Our commitment to your privacy

We collect only information relevant to our business activities, explain why it is needed, and handle it responsibly. This page describes the systems the website uses now: the three forms on this site, the intake store behind them, the consent record your own browser keeps, and the two font and icon files named in the Cookie Policy. No analytics service and no advertising tracker run here.

01 Who We Are

Kwetu Creations provides creative services including signage, branding, interior solutions, illuminated signage and fabrication.

For this policy, references to "we", "us" and "our" mean Kwetu Creations. References to "you" mean a visitor, prospective client or client using our website or contacting us.

Our business contact details should be provided below before this policy is published.

02 Information We Collect

Depending on how you interact with our website, we may collect the following categories of information when necessary for a specific purpose.

Contact and business details

  • Your name and business or organisation name.
  • Email address and telephone number.
  • Business address or project location, where relevant.

Quotation and project information

  • The service you are interested in.
  • Project descriptions, measurements and requirements.
  • Budgets, preferred timelines and other details you choose to provide.
  • Artwork, logos, photographs, reference images and other files you upload or send to us.

What the forms on this website ask for

  • Quote request: full name, business or organisation name, email address, contact number, business address, the service required, a project description, and any files you attach.
  • Contact form: full name, company name, email address, phone number, the subject of your enquiry and your message.
  • Testimonial form: your name, business name, rating, the testimonial text and the publication permission you tick.

Where those answers go. All three forms send their contents to this website's own intake system, which stores them in a database on the server that runs the site. A quote request and its attachments appear in a private review screen that only someone with the studio's review password can open; nothing you type becomes visible to another visitor. A testimonial is published on the website only after a person reads it and approves it, and only if you ticked the permission box. The site does not reply to you automatically — answering is done by a person, from the studio's own phone or email address. If you would rather not use the form, the phone number and email address on the contact page work directly.

What these forms do not ask for

None of the forms asks for a password, payment card, national ID, date of birth or any other sensitive identifier, and you should not volunteer those in a message or a file.

Basic technical information

Our hosting provider or website systems may record technical information such as browser type, device information, access times and server logs.

The intake system itself keeps one technical value alongside each submission: a one-way fingerprint of the IP address your device submitted from, used only to cap how many enquiries arrive from the same address in a day. The address itself is not stored, and the fingerprint is salted, so it is not turned back into an address or matched against other websites. The form also notes how long the page was open before you pressed submit, which exists to make automated submissions harder, not to watch you.

Please do not submit sensitive personal information through our general enquiry or quotation forms unless it is genuinely necessary and we have explained how it will be handled.

03 How We Use Information

We use personal information only for legitimate, relevant business purposes, which are:

  • Responding to enquiries and quotation requests.
  • Understanding your project requirements.
  • Preparing estimates, designs and proposals.
  • Communicating about project progress, approvals and delivery.
  • Maintaining appropriate business and transaction records.
  • Reading a testimonial and deciding whether to publish it.
  • Protecting our website and preventing misuse.
  • Meeting applicable legal obligations.

One mechanical use is worth naming: when an enquiry or a testimonial is stored, the intake system can send a plain text copy of what you typed to the studio's own mailboxes, so that somebody notices it without having to log in and look. That step is switched off until a real mail account is configured, and it is described again under Sharing Information below.

We will not treat a quotation enquiry as permission to send unrelated promotional messages. If we introduce marketing communications, we will provide an appropriate way to manage your preferences.

04 Uploaded Design Files

Clients may be asked to provide logos, artwork, reference photographs, technical drawings or other files to help us understand and complete a project.

We use those files to:

  • Review your project requirements.
  • Prepare quotations and design proposals.
  • Complete agreed design, production or installation work.
  • Resolve project-related questions where necessary.

Please upload only files you are authorised to share. Do not upload confidential material that is unrelated to your project.

Attachments on the quote request form are limited to four files of up to 8 MB each, in the image, document, archive and drawing formats the form lists. Each file is checked against its own contents, not merely its name, and a file whose contents do not match the format it claims is refused — in which case the whole request is refused, so you are never told a drawing arrived when it did not.

Accepted files are saved on the same server, under a randomly generated name, in a folder that no web address can reach. They are opened only by someone signed in to the private review screen, and the browser is forced to treat them as a download rather than a page, which is what stops a crafted file from running anything on your machine or ours. Your original file name is kept so the studio can recognise it; the copy on the server carries a different name.

Delete the request and its files go with it. Until then, attachments stay on the server for as long as the enquiry they belong to.

We will not use a client's confidential artwork for unrelated purposes. Any use of completed work in our portfolio or promotional materials should be handled separately and with the appropriate permission where required.

05 Sharing Information

We do not sell personal information to third parties.

Information may need to be shared in limited circumstances, such as:

  • With authorised team members working on your project.
  • With hosting, email, file-storage or other service providers when those services are used to operate the website or fulfil your request.
  • Where disclosure is required by applicable law or a lawful request.
  • Where necessary to protect rights, property or security, subject to applicable law.

The one internal route worth spelling out: if email notification is switched on, the text of your enquiry — the same fields listed above — is delivered to mailboxes the studio controls, through the mail service the studio has configured, with your email address set as the reply address so a reply goes back to you. Attached files are not emailed; they stay on the server and are read there. If a mail send fails, the failure is a line in our server log and nothing further leaves our system.

Those providers are used only to operate this website and to answer the request you make through it. If the arrangements for sharing information change, this page is updated and the published version is the one that applies.

Some providers may process information outside Uganda. Any such arrangement must be assessed against the applicable data-protection requirements.

06 Data Security

We use reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect personal information against unauthorised access, disclosure, loss, alteration or misuse.

On the intake system specifically, those measures are:

  • The review screens sit behind a password stored only as a one-way hash, and repeat failures to guess it are counted and locked out.
  • Submitted text reaches the database through prepared statements, so what you type is treated as data and never as instructions.
  • Every submission is checked for a token issued to your own browser session, for the page it came from, for a hidden field a person never fills in, and for how long the form was open before submit.
  • Uploads are verified against their own contents, renamed by the server, and stored in a folder no web address can reach; the review screen hands them back only as forced downloads.
  • The configuration file that holds database and mail credentials is not readable through the website.

No website or electronic storage system can guarantee absolute security. Two limitations are stated plainly rather than hidden: before this site is served over HTTPS, anything sent to it — including a review password — travels in the open, which is why the review screens must not be used from a shared network until encryption is in place; and a submission is stored on one server, so a compromise of that server is a compromise of what it holds. Backups, once they exist, will be covered by the same access rules.

07 How Long We Keep Information

We retain personal information only for as long as it is reasonably necessary for the purpose for which it was collected, or for any longer period required or permitted by applicable law.

Retention periods may differ for unsuccessful enquiries, completed projects, financial records and legal records.

What runs today, stated as it is: a stored enquiry stays in the database until a person with the review password marks it handled, archives it, or deletes it. Archiving hides it from the default view but does not erase it. Deleting removes the record and its attached files at the same moment, and there is no undo. An approved testimonial stays on the public website until someone removes it.

Nothing is deleted automatically: no expiry job runs, so an old enquiry is not cleared just because it is old. Send us a request from the contact details below and a person will delete your submission and its attached files.

08 Cookies, Storage and Analytics

Cookies are small files that a website may store on your device. Websites also use browser storage such as localStorage, which is treated the same way under cookie rules, and some sites use analytics tools to understand how visitors interact with their pages.

What this website stores itself: one entry in localStorage that records the choice you make in the consent bar — which categories you accepted, the version of the policy you answered and the date. It stays on your device, is never uploaded by this website, and exists only so the banner does not reopen on every page you visit. In addition, pressing submit on one of the three forms makes our server set a single cookie (kwetu_intake) that identifies your own submission to the server so it can confirm the request started here. It holds a random number, not anything you typed, and it expires when you close the browser. If you only read pages, neither of these is written.

No analytics and no advertising run here. This website uses no measurement service, no advertising cookie and no social-platform tracker, and it does not report your visit to any of them. A consent bar appears on your first visit with three options: Accept All, Reject Non-Essential and Cookie Settings, which separates Necessary, Analytics and Marketing. Your answer for the last two is stored on your device and honoured: neither of them loads anything, because this website has nothing in those categories to load. The Cookie Policy lists what is set, and what is not.

Requests that are not cookies: every page asks Google Fonts for the Poppins typeface and the cdnjs content delivery network for the icon stylesheet, and a mailto: link opens your own email application. Those requests carry your IP address to the provider, which handles them under its own privacy policy. They are not used for advertising or profiling, and the Cookie Policy lists them in full.

Before any analytics service is connected, its purpose, the information it collects and the controls required by applicable law will be reviewed, and this policy, the Cookie Policy and the consent wording will be updated on the same day the service goes live.

Your consent choice can be reopened at any time from Cookie Settings in the footer of every page. Your browser can also block or delete cookies and site data, although blocking everything means the consent bar may reappear on each visit because the answer cannot be remembered.

Full details, including the storage inventory, are in the Cookie Policy.

09 Your Privacy Rights

Subject to applicable law and any relevant conditions or exceptions, you may have rights concerning your personal information, including the right to:

  • Ask whether we hold personal information about you.
  • Request access to your information.
  • Request correction of inaccurate or incomplete information.
  • Request deletion where applicable and legally permitted.
  • Object to certain processing or request that it stop where the law provides that right.
  • Raise a concern about how your information has been handled.

To make a request, contact Kwetu Creations using the details in the Contact Us section. We may need to verify your identity before acting on a request.

If you sent us something through a form and want it gone, say which submission — the name you used and roughly when you sent it is enough to find it. The review screen can delete an enquiry and its attachments outright, and a testimonial can be taken down from the website, in both cases by whoever moderates it. Deletion is carried out by a person rather than by a button on the page, so that the record being erased is confirmed first.

We will consider and respond to requests in accordance with applicable law. Some records may need to be retained where a legal obligation or another lawful basis applies.
