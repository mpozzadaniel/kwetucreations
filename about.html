Who We Are

Creative Ideas. Real-World Solutions.

Kwetu Creations is a creative company founded by Stephen Were Mbatya in 2014 and focused on helping businesses express their identity and bring ideas into the real world.

The studio works from Kampala and stays with a project from the first concept through to installation.

We work across branding, signage, illuminated signage, fabrication, interior solutions and website design. These areas allow us to approach projects from both a visual and a practical perspective.

We believe good creative work begins with understanding the client. By listening, planning carefully and paying attention to the details, we aim to deliver solutions that suit each project's purpose.

Our Philosophy

More Than Appearance. Built Around Purpose.

We believe creative work should do more than look good. It should communicate clearly, serve a purpose and make sense for the people who will use it.

A brand identity should communicate who a business is. Signage should help people recognise and navigate a place. An interior solution should consider how a space will be experienced.

This way of thinking guides how we approach the projects entrusted to us: with attention to the brief, the details and the intended result.
